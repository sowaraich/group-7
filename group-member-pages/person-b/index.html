DANIEL EKONDE

Eight years of storytelling

MY PROFILE

Daniel Ekonde is journalist and second-year graduate student at UC Berkeley California. He specializes in narrative and multimedia journalism, his toolkit includes data reporting with R Programming, video production with Adobe Suite, photography, and web publishing through WordPress, CSS, and HTML.He holds a Bachelor of Science in Broadcast Journalism from the University of Buea, Cameroon, completed in June 2018. He is bilingual, with fluency in English and proficiency in French across written, reading, and conversational contexts.

Before coming to UC Berkeley, Daniel worked as a TV sports journalist at the Cameroon Radio Television (CRTV), where he reported, anchored, and commentated major events including the 2022 FIFA World Cup and the 2021 Africa Cup of Nations. He was awarded the Best TV Sports Reporter distinction by the Cameroon English Language Newspaper Publishers Association for his "insightful" reports.

Daniel has reported for a range of local and international outlets, including Al Jazeera, CNN, Quartz, Semafor, Berkeleyside, and Africa is a Country. As an editorial intern at Berkeleyside in the summer of 2025, he produced two 1,200-word stories per week, covering beats ranging from science and business to city hall, with one story on UC Berkeley students securing affordable housing attracting 12,000 views within a week.

Daniel is an active member of the journalism community, serving as Secretary of the National Association of Black Journalists (NABJ) UC Berkeley Charter and as a member of the Society for Professional Journalists. He is also a journalism mentor with CamerToday and a contributing member of Global Voices.
