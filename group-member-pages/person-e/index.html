My Honest Review of Butter & Crumble Bakery

Recently, I became a social media "sheep" and went to "Butter & Crumble."

It's located in San Fracncisco, just a Bart ride away. But the bakery however, took 3 hours. We got 4 different pastries, a savory pesto twist, a baklava, a biscoff croissant, and a coffee cruffin. Would I do it again? Probably not. Was it delicious? Yes!

10/10
