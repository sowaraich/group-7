A little something about me

My name is Jie Zhang, though some people also know me as Jerry. I am a UC Berkeley MDes student interested in design, technology, and experiences that feel just a little off. I am fascinated by the uncanny: the moment when something appears familiar, but carries a quiet sense of wrongness beneath the surface.

I enjoy soccer, video games, motorcycles, traveling, adventure, and exploring the unknown. I’m especially interested in experiences that feel exciting, mysterious, and a little unusual.
